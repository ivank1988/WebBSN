About us

Business Systems Network.

We connect business processes, people and technology, helping companies choose, implement and grow the systems they run on.

BSN stands for Business Systems Network. We believe great technology should feel simple, and work quietly in the background of a successful business.

We implement Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and other ERP systems, bring AI and Microsoft Copilot into everyday work, and build web and mobile applications and websites.

Our main markets are Ireland and Croatia, with headquarters in Dublin and Zagreb. Over the years we have worked with clients all over the world.
